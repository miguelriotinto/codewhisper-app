# Code[Whisper] Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026.

Code[Whisper] is built so that your voice and your words stay yours. **We don't collect any data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us.

## On your device

- **Speech** is transcribed on your device with Apple's speech models. Audio is never sent anywhere.
- **Your dictations, History, words, snippets, styles, and settings** are stored on your device.

## iCloud

If iCloud is on, your words, snippets, styles, and settings sync between your own devices through your private iCloud account, run by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We can't see this data. History stays on each device.

## Cloud styles (optional)

Some styles can rewrite a dictation with a cloud model. This is off until you turn it on and enter your own API key for a provider you choose: Anthropic, OpenAI, or Amazon Bedrock. Then, when a dictation uses such a style, its **text** (never audio) is sent directly from your device to that provider to be rewritten, under that provider's terms and privacy policy. Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain. We don't receive the text or the key.

## The keyboard

The Code[Whisper] keyboard asks for Full Access only to exchange the dictation with the Code[Whisper] app on your device. The keyboard has no network access, doesn't record what you type, and doesn't send anything off your device.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** to hear what you dictate, only while a session is on, shown by iOS's recording indicator and the Live Activity.
- **Speech recognition:** to transcribe on your device.

## Children

Code[Whisper] doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
