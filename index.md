# Code[Whisper] Support

Code[Whisper] lets you dictate into any app with the Code[Whisper] keyboard. Speech is transcribed on your device.

Questions, problems, or ideas: write to **[miguel.rio.tinto@gmail.com](mailto:miguel.rio.tinto@gmail.com)**. We usually reply within two working days.

## Getting started

1. Open Code[Whisper] and follow the four setup steps.
2. Add the keyboard: **Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard > Code[Whisper]**, then turn on **Allow Full Access**. Full Access lets the keyboard talk to the Code[Whisper] app on your device; the keyboard has no network access and doesn't record what you type.
3. Allow the microphone and download the speech model for your language when the app asks.

## Dictating

- **Start a session** from Control Center, the Lock Screen, or the Action button (add the **Start Session** control), or from Siri. The session listens in the background with a Live Activity, and you stay in your app.
- In any text field, switch to the Code[Whisper] keyboard with the 🌐 key and tap the mic. Tap it again to stop; the text is inserted where you were typing.
- If no session is running, the mic opens Code[Whisper] once to start one. Swipe right along the bottom edge of the screen to go back to your app.
- End a session with **End** on the Live Activity, or in the app. It also ends by itself after a few minutes without a dictation (Settings > Session).

## Styles, words, and languages

- **Styles** clean up and format what you say (Plain, Formal, Casual, and more). Full rewrites such as Code Prompt use a cloud model from Anthropic, OpenAI, or Amazon Bedrock with your own API key (Settings > Cloud).
- **Words** teaches Code[Whisper] names and terms, and snippets you can say to insert longer text.
- **Languages**: dictate in English or Portuguese (Portugal) without switching, with **Also Detect** in Settings > Languages.

## Troubleshooting

- **The keyboard says to turn on Full Access:** Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Code[Whisper] > Allow Full Access.
- **Nothing happens when I tap the mic:** open Code[Whisper] and check Home says the session is on, and that the microphone is allowed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.
- **Switch back to another keyboard:** tap 🌐, or hold it to pick from the list.
